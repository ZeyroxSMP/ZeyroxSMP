READ BEFORE YOU PLAY

FAIR PLAY ONLY

PLAY SMART.
PLAY FAIR.

These Velo SMP rules are inspired by the general categories used by large survival servers, including DonutSMP, but written in our own words. Velo SMP is not affiliated with DonutSMP.

01 / CLIENT & GAMEPLAY

Keep the game
fair for everyone.

If a mod or tool gives you an unfair advantage, do not use it on Velo.

  • 01No hacked clients or cheats.
  • 02No X-ray, ESP, player radar, freecam, or cave/ore-revealing tools.
  • 03No movement, inventory, health-indicator, auto-place, or similar advantage mods.
  • 04No macros, scripts, auto-clickers, or automated farming/combat.
  • 05No abusing glitches, attempting to duplicate items, or keeping duplicated items.
  • 06Report bugs and exploits to the server owner or staff.

02 / ITEMS & TRADING

Earn it.
Trade honestly.

Keep trades fair and do not exploit other players.

  • 07No buying or selling in-game items, currency, or accounts for real-world money.
  • 08No trading items or currency between Velo and other servers.
  • 09No scamming or misleading players in a deal.
  • 10No exploiting shops, economy systems, or bugs for unfair profit.
  • 11No offering in-game rewards for outside promotions unless staff allows it.

03 / CHAT & COMMUNITY

Respect the
whole community.

Keep Velo welcoming, even when competition gets intense.

  • 12No spam, harassment, bullying, threats, or hate speech.
  • 13No sharing private or identifying information about yourself or others.
  • 14No offensive names, skins, builds, signs, or map art.
  • 15No pretending to be staff or bypassing punishments with alternate accounts.
  • 16No advertising other servers, cheats, or services without permission.
  • 17Follow staff instructions and use the proper channel to report issues or appeal a punishment.
Enforcement: Staff may issue a warning, mute, temporary ban, or permanent ban depending on the situation. Rules may change, and attempts to bypass them still count as violations. Confirm these as the final official rules before opening the server.

KEEP IT FAIR. KEEP IT FUN.
