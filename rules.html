READ BEFORE PLAYING

KEEP THE WORLD FAIR

NO CHEATS.
NO DRAMA.

These are suggested community rules for Velo SMP, based on common survival-server standards. The server owner or staff may add official rules and decide how penalties work.

RULE 01 / FAIR PLAY

No hacks or cheats

No hacked clients, x-ray, combat automation, unfair macros, or tools that give an unfair advantage.

RULE 02 / EXPLOITS

No bug abuse

Do not abuse glitches, duplication bugs, or unintended mechanics. Report serious bugs to staff.

RULE 03 / RESPECT

Respect every player

No harassment, hate speech, threats, or targeting people to make them feel unsafe.

RULE 04 / CHAT

No spam or advertising

Do not flood chat, impersonate staff, or advertise other servers without permission.

RULE 05 / HONESTY

No scamming

If trading is enabled, keep deals honest. Do not trick players out of items or currency.

RULE 06 / BUILDS

No griefing or stealing

Do not destroy or take another player's things without permission. Follow any posted PvP and raiding rules.

RULE 07 / ACCOUNTS

No ban evasion

Do not use alternate accounts to bypass a punishment or continue rule-breaking.

RULE 08 / STAFF

Listen to staff

Follow reasonable staff instructions and use the correct channel to appeal a punishment.

RULE 09 / COMMON SENSE

Don't ruin the fun

Do not intentionally crash the server, disrupt events, or abuse other players.

Important: These rules are a draft for your website, not verified official rules from DonutSMP or your hosting panel. Before publishing them as official, confirm what rules you want and what your server actually allows. Staff should explain penalties clearly and apply rules fairly.

UNDERSTAND THE RULES? GOOD. NOW HAVE FUN.
